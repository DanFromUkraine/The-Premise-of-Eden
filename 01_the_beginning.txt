"What I call Syntetic Intelligence"

We already have large language models, machine learning models. I would expect a question, why do we need even larger vocabulary, "let's just call everything artificial intelligence". However, all this development of tools, we are using everyday makes me ask a lot of questions, and of them: what is the difference between us and LLM? I will explain it a bit below, but for now: what if create a computer with a dynamic algorithms of constant learning that functionally and architectually doesn't have any difference with us? It acts unpredictably, emotionally, the way not a single forecasting model can produce. It something beyond the lexicon we use nowadays, as now such technologies are non-existant. Now let me introduce the things you most likely already know, but I just want to stay on the same page with you: in computer world we usually have only simulation, and emulation. Simulation, is when you only mimic the outer charasterisitcs and behavior, while emulation reproduces all the steps and actions original system did in the same way, so outer charasteristics appear on their own. Here is a short example: you take photo of a brain, where you can see neurons in high-res, somehow, then you ask pretrained machine learning model to predict, how it will look like ONLY VISUALLY, in about 1 hour. On the other hand, instead of taking a picture you form a complex data structure of neurons, all their physical connections, peptide ones, all this chemical soup they are floating in, so you mathematically try to calculate how neurons will grow, activate, etc. The former, visual method, is surely simulation, while the second one is emulation. This understanding is really important, because all LLM modals only mimic the symptoms of behavior of humans who created the data they consumed, and while they have weights in their memory, so they can relate concepts of male and female closer compared to male and apple, but it's only one of the core mechanisms we have in our mind out of dozens. The prerequisites are done, so let's go back to the syntetic intelligence. This term was created by the philosopher John Haugeland in 1985. Basically, if AI is a simulation, then syntetic intelligence is an emulation. He drew a novel comparison between AI and SI: AI is like cubic zirconia - a mere imitation - whereas SI is like a lab-grown diamond, which is chemically authentic. I believe now this SI also sticks to your mind, and you will own yet another acronim.
